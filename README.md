## Introduction

As technology continues to grow and improve over the course of time, so does analysis and statistics in sports. There are new adavanced statistics used every year in sports because of the new technology and advanced frameworks data scientists use for athletes. In baseball there are many different metrics used to determine how an individual player contributes to specific areas of the game. For example, some batters are used in baseball games simply because they might have a better hitting average against a right handed pitcher than their own teammate. There are multiple analytical aspects that managers look upon to determine which of their players should be played in a baseball game. These statistics and trends can influence the results of games as well as the future financial outcomes for players and organizations.

The outcome of baseball players performances can offer promising financial opportunities that significantly impact baseball organizations. When batters succeed and post high quality statistics throughout a season, they are offered lucrative contracts by organizations at the end of the year. For some organizations, this is presents great opportunity, but it also includes serious risks. If an organization offers an older player with strong quality statistics an expensive contract, they risk investing in someone who might have reached their maximum playing potential. This is important for smaller market organizations as if they do spend most of their budget on one single player then they might not have the budget to spend on more players. This issue has an impact on having winning games in a season as well as financial success for organizations. If a player who recieves a lengthy expensive contract does not meet expectations, ticket sales, merchandise, and organizational value will potentially leading to an owner selling or relocating the team.

As a former collegiate athelete and sports fanatic, I understand the organizational impacts of offering players lengthy expensive contracts. I want to investigate the relationship between age and batting statistics for players in Major League Baseball. Recognizing trends and patterns could help managers of organizations understand what the ideal age is to sign a player to lengthy contract worth millions of dollars. This research will help managers and owners making healthier decisions for their organizations that will help with all of their finances.
