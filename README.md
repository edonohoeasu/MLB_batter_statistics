## Introduction

As technology continues to grow and improve over the course of time, so does analysis and statistics in sports. There are new adavanced statistics used every year in sports because of the new technology and advanced frameworks data scientists use for athletes. In baseball there are many different metrics used to determine how an individual player contributes to specific areas of the game. For example, some batters are used in baseball games simply because they might have a better hitting average against a right handed pitcher than their own teammate. There are multiple analytical aspects that managers look upon to determine which of their players should be played in a baseball game. These statistics and trends can influence the results of games as well as the future financial outcomes for players and organizations.
